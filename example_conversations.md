# 3 Example Conversations

## Example 1 — Jaipur

### User

I want to visit Jaipur for 3 days with a budget of ₹15,000. I like history and local food.

### Agent

## Trip Summary
- Destination: Jaipur
- Duration: 3 days
- Budget: ₹15,000
- Interests: History and local food
- Assumption: Local Jaipur travel only; travel to/from Jaipur is excluded.

## Recommended Places
- Amber Fort — major historical attraction.
- City Palace — royal history and architecture.
- Jantar Mantar — UNESCO-listed astronomical observatory.
- Hawa Mahal — iconic Jaipur architecture.
- Johari/Bapu Bazaar area — local shopping and food.

## Estimated Budget
- Accommodation: about ₹5,400
- Food: about ₹2,400
- Local transport: about ₹1,500
- Activities: about ₹1,200
- Estimated local total: about ₹10,500
- Remaining budget: about ₹4,500

## Day-wise Plan
### Day 1
- Morning: Amber Fort
- Afternoon: Jaigarh area / lunch
- Evening: Hawa Mahal and nearby markets
- Food: Dal baati churma

### Day 2
- Morning: City Palace
- Afternoon: Jantar Mantar
- Evening: Old City walk and local market
- Food: Pyaz kachori and lassi

### Day 3
- Morning: Albert Hall Museum
- Afternoon: relaxed shopping/food exploration
- Evening: local dinner and departure preparation
- Food: Ghewar or traditional Rajasthani thali
